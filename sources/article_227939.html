Fe₃O₄-NH₂/GO an effective modifier for simultaneous voltammetric detection of isoniazid in the presence of acetaminophen

Document Type : Original Article

Authors

Fatemeh Ebrahimi-Tazangi 1
Jamileh Seyed-Yazdi 1
Hadi Beitollahi 2

1 Department of Physics, Faculty of Science, Vali-e-Asr University of Rafsanjan, Rafsanjan, Iran

2 Environment Department, Institute of Science and High Technology and Environmental Sciences, Graduate University of Advanced Technology, Kerman, Iran

https://doi.org/10.48309/ajnm.2025.531917.1000
Abstract
This study describes a simple and cost-effective procedure to develop a new electrochemical sensor to simultaneously detect acetaminophen (AC) and isoniazid (INZ). We present the study of a screen-printed graphite electrode (SPGE) modified with Fe3O4-NH2/GO nanocomposite by differential pulse voltammetry (DPV), chronoamperometry (CHA), and cyclic voltammetry (CV). Additionally, we used X-ray diffraction (XRD), vibrating sample magnetometry (VSM), Fourier-transform infrared spectroscopy (FTIR), energy-dispersive X-ray spectroscopy (EDS), and field emission scanning electron microscopy (FESEM) to characterize the synthesized nanocomposites. The DPV peak current of INZ increases linearly with a concentration in the range 0.07-460.0 µM and its limit of detection (LOD) is 0.02±0.001 µM. The results show that the modified electrode exhibits robust functions to circumvent the problem of overlapping the two voltammetric peaks of INZ and AC. We demonstrate the interest in using Fe3O4-NH2/GO/SPGE to detect INZ and AC in real samples.

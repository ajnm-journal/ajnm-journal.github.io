Bisphenol a: Analysis using modified graphite screen printed electrode as an electrochemical sensor

Document Type : Original Article

Authors

Mahdi Adeli Nejad 1
Hamid Sarhadi 1
Erfan Sarhadi 2

1 Department of Food Science, Islamic Azad University, Bam.C., Bam, Iran

2 Faculty of Pharmacy, Kerman University of Medical Sciences, Kerman, Iran

https://doi.org/10.48309/ajnm.2025.537230.1013
Abstract
The La2O3/Co3O4 nanocomposite modified graphite screen printed electrode (La2O3/Co3O4/GSPE) was used to create an electrochemical sensor for the electrochemical measurement of bisphenol A. Through the use of SEM, the La2O3/Co3O4 nanocomposite was characterized. Voltammetry was employed to study the electrochemical properties of bisphenol A. The electrode modification caused a notable shift in the peak potential (~220 mV) and a significant rise in the anodic peak current (~2.5 times). The oxidation peak current of bisphenol A fluctuated linearly with concentration under ideal circumstances, spanning a broad range of 0.5 to 450.0 µM. The detection limit of this technique was as low as 0.15 µM. The proposed technique was also successfully applied to identify bisphenol A in actual samples.

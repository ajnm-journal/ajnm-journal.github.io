Synergistic investigation of hexagonal α-MnTe phase synthesized via electrochemical deposition technique for photovoltaic application

Document Type : Original Article

Authors

Blessing U. Osolobri 1
Ernest O. Ojegu 2
Imosobomeh Lucky Ikhioya 3

1 Department of Science Laboratory Technology, Delta State Polytechnic, Ogwashi – Uku. Delta State, Nigeria

2 Department of Physics, Delta State University, Abraka. Delta State, Nigeria

3 Department of Physics, Federal University Lokoja, Kogi State, Nigeria

https://doi.org/10.48309/ajnm.2026.575456.1032
Abstract
Manganese telluride (MnTe) is another promising p-type semiconductor for photovoltaic (PV) systems, noted for its high absorption coefficient and tunable band gaps. MnTe thin films in this paper, was deposited on the fluorine-doped tin oxide (FTO) substrate through an electrochemical deposition process. X-ray diffraction (XRD) was used to analyze the structure and supported the standard lattice planes with the hexagonal α-MnTe phase with diffraction peaks of between 25.3o and 75.3o. Morphology on the surface indicated that the early deposition produced single islands, which caused a rough surface. Nevertheless, with the deposition duration increased to 40 seconds, the grain coalescence, and a more homogeneous film is formed. Additional optimization of the deposition time of 50 seconds however increased the thickness of the film and the grain size and improved the maximum absorbance within the UV-visible and near-infrared (NIR) spectra. This expansion also increased the light-trapping ability, which is essential in enhancing high density of current during short circuit. Direct band gap is 1.79 eV, and such electrodeposited MnTe films present a high potential in top-cell absorber in tandem solar cell arrangements on a substrate with lower band gaps.

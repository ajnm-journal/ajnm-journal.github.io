Electrochemical determination of methyldopa using modified screen printed carbon electrode

Document Type : Original Article

Authors

Mohammad Reza Aflatoonian 1
Somayeh Tajik 2

1 Leishmaniasis Research Center, Kerman University of Medical Sciences, Kerman, Iran

2 Research Center of Tropical and Infectious Diseases, Kerman University of Medical Sciences, Kerman, Iran

https://doi.org/10.48309/ajnm.2025.534746.1008
Abstract
This study aimed to design and build an electrochemical sensing platform for measuring methyldopa (MDP). To do this, a nanocomposite of CoWO4 nanoparticles (NPs) anchored to reduced grapheme oxide nanosheets (rGO NSs) was made using a straightforward hydrothermal method. FE-SEM techniques were employed to verify the synthesis of the CoWO4/rGO nanocomposite (CWRGs). The produced CWRGs were then used to modify the surface of a screen-printed carbon electrode (SPCE) to enhance MDP determination. The outstanding electrochemical response of the modified SPCE (CWRGs/SPCE) to the MDP oxidation was investigated using cyclic voltammetry (CV) due to the advantageous properties of CoWO4 NPs and rGO NSs as well as their complimentary effects. For MDP determination, the CWRGs/SPCE sensor showed a low limit of detection (LOD) of 0.1±0.01 μM and a wide linear detection range of 0.3 to 180.0 μM under ideal circumstances. Additionally, CWRGs/SPCE had a sensitivity of 0.1156 μA/μM for MDP detection. Furthermore, the possibility for detecting this molecule in actual samples was demonstrated by the excellent recoveries for detecting spiked MDP in the MDP tablet and urine samples at the CWRGs/SPCE surface.

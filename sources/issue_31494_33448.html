Application of a modified graphite screen-printed electrode for sensitive detection of vitamin C

Pages 233-240

https://doi.org/10.48309/ajnm.2025.537234.1014

Hamid Sarhadi, Sajedeh Moshrefi, Batul Rezaei Zadeh

Abstract Vitamin C, is a water-soluble vitamin that is found in varying amounts in a wide variety of fruits and vegetables. It is widely used in the food, chemical, and pharmaceutical industries. In this study, a quick, easy, affordable, and efficient electrochemical method was developed for detecting vitamin C. The electrochemical signal of vitamin C was significantly increased at the La2O3/Co3O4 nanocomposite-modified graphite screen-printed electrode (La2O3/Co3O4/GSPE). SEM, or scanning electron microscopy, was used to clarify the surface characterization clearer. Using differential pulse voltammetry, the limit of detection was determined to be 0.2 ± 0.01 µM with a good regression between the concentration and peak current of vitamin C (R2=0.9993) within the range of 0.7 to 450.0 µM. The modified electrode was effectively used to accurately measure vitamin C in actual samples.

A Novel Ni(II) nano-complex with schiff base ligand: Synthesis, spectroscopic characterization and study of antibacterial properties

Pages 241-249

https://doi.org/10.48309/ajnm.2025.561983.1022

Mohsen Darvishbeigi, Niloufar Akbarzadeh-T

Abstract A new mononuclear Ni(II) nanocomplex with the formula [Ni(L)2] (a) (L = 2-hydroxy-1-naphthaldehyde-2-aminothiophenol) has been synthesized by a sonochemical process. The nanocomplex (a) was characterized using FT-IR, UV-Vis, elemental analysis, single-crystal X-ray diffraction and scanning electron microscopy (SEM). The average size of the nanocomplex synthesized via the sonochemical method was approximately 52 nm. Spectroscopic analyses revealed that the two Schiff base ligands form an octahedral geometry through the oxygen, sulfur and nitrogen atoms. The antibacterial behavior of the synthesized compound was investigated for two groups of gram-positive members of Staphylococcus aureus (S. aureus ATCC 25923) and Enterococcus faecalis (Enter_faeca ATCC 29212) and two gram-negative members of Escherichia coli (E. coli ATCC 25922) and Pseudomonas aeruginosa (P. aeruginosa ATCC 27853). In this study, the inhibition method and the dilution method were used to determine the minimum inhibitory concentration (MIC) and the minimum bactericidal concentration (MBC), and the results showed that the synthesized complex is active against bacteria.

Rational design of CNT-based nanocarriers for cancer therapy: An atomistic perspective on doxorubicin loading with –COOH and –OH groups

Pages 250-263

https://doi.org/10.48309/ajnm.2025.549645.1017

Arefeh Esmaeili, Mahshid Ahmadi

Abstract Doxorubicin (DOX) is a potent chemotherapeutic agent with broad efficacy against various malignancies; however, its clinical use is restricted by dose-dependent cardiotoxicity and other systemic side effects. Carbon nanotubes (CNTs) have emerged as promising nanocarriers due to their high surface area, tunable surface chemistry, and potential to improve drug delivery efficiency. In this study, the adsorption and stabilization of DOX onto CNTs functionalized with –COOH and –OH groups were systematically examined using all-atom molecular dynamics simulations. Structural and energetic analyses—including potential and total energies, RMSD, contact number, center-of-mass distance, hydrogen bonding, non-bonded interaction energies, radius of gyration (Rg), solvent accessible surface area (SASA), and radial distribution function (RDF)—confirmed the structural stability and energetic consistency of the CNT–DOX complex under physiological conditions. Van der Waals forces were found to dominate the binding process, complemented by electrostatic and hydrogen-bonding interactions that enhance drug retention. The decrease in Rg and SASA indicated the formation of a compact and solvent-shielded structure, while RDF analysis showed preferential localization of DOX within ~1.0 nm of the CNT surface. These findings reveal the superior loading capacity and stability of functionalized CNTs and provide atomistic insights into their potential as efficient, biocompatible nanocarriers for targeted anticancer drug delivery.

Voltammetric determination of sulfanilamide as a medicinal drug using Fe3O4@CuO-RGO modified screen printed electrode

Pages 264-278

https://doi.org/10.48309/ajnm.2025.555747.1020

Fatemeh Irannezhad, Jamileh Seyed-Yazdi

Abstract Sulfonamides (Such as sulfanilamide) are a kind of broad-spectrum antimicrobial drugs, used in livestock, poultry and aquaculture. Their residues in food may cause potential harm to consumers' health. Therefore, it is necessary to develop a simple, rapid and high-sensitivity detection method. In this investigation, graphite oxide (GO) was synthesized via a modified Hummer’s method to ensure enhanced oxidation efficiency. Iron oxide (Fe₃O₄) nanoparticles were prepared through a co-precipitation technique, followed by the fabrication of the Fe₃O₄@CuO-reduced graphene oxide (RGO) composite via a solvothermal procedure. Comprehensive characterization of the synthesized materials was conducted employing Fourier Transform Infrared Spectroscopy (FTIR), X-ray Diffraction (XRD), Field Emission Scanning Electron Microscopy coupled with Energy Dispersive X-ray Spectroscopy (FE-SEM/EDX), and Transmission Electron Microscopy (TEM). These analyses confirmed the successful synthesis of the composite and provided substantive evidence of the proposed core-shell architecture of Fe₃O₄@CuO, highlighting its structural integrity and morphological features. Then, an electrochemical sensing method for detecting sulfonamide was established. Firstly, bare and modified electrode were screened using cyclic voltammetry(CV). The results showed that sulfonamide on magnetite (Fe3O4) @ CuO nanomaterials (CuO) - Reduced Graphene oxide (RGO) modified electrode had the largest response. The linear regression equation for modified electrode was y=0.352x+0.3753 (R2=0.9995) with the detection limit 0.03 µM. In this study, an electrode modification material with high sensitivity to sulfonamide was selected, and a simple, rapid and stable electrochemical detection method was established. This method has good applicability for determining sulfonamide drug residues in urine sample.

Reduced graphene oxide/ZnS nanowires modified screen printed electrode for the quantification of medicinal drug calcium folinate in injection samples

Pages 279-289

https://doi.org/10.48309/ajnm.2025.562139.1023

Reza Zaimbashi, Ahlam Bazrafkan

Abstract The research reports the development of a new type of modified screen-printed electrode (SPE) incorporating reduced graphene oxide and ZnS nanowires (rGO/ZnS NWs) for highly sensitive electrochemical detection of calcium folinate (CF). The behavior of the rGO/ZnS NWs-based SPE was investigated using cyclic voltammetry (CV), chronoamperometry, and differential pulse voltammetry (DPV). Relative to the bare SPE, the modi-fied electrode demonstrated markedly enhanced electrocatalytic proper-ties and a larger electroactive surface. Under optimized conditions, the rGO/ZnS NWs/SPE provided a wide linear response range (0.2–350.0 μM) along with a very low limit of detection (LOD=0.06 μM). In addition, it successfully measured calcium folinate in injection samples, yielding excellent recovery values (97.8–103.3%). Overall, the results highlight the suitability of the rGO/ZnS NWs-modified SPE as a fast, economical, and dependable tool for electroanalytical applications in pharmaceutical analysis.

Original Article

Electrocatalytic oxidation of medicinal drug sertraline at a graphitic carbon nitride nanosheets and Zn-Co zeolitic imidazolate framework modified glassy carbon electrode

Pages 290-299

https://doi.org/10.48309/ajnm.2025.565026.1025

Peyman Jahani, Maedeh Jafari

Abstract As a novel electrochemical platform for sertraline detection, a glassy carbon electrode modified with graphitic carbon nitride nanosheets (g-C3N4) and Zn-Co zeolitic imidazolate framework (Zn-Co ZIF) (g-C3N4/Zn-Co ZIF/GCE) is suggested. Using cyclic voltammetry (CV) studies in 0.1 M phosphate buffer solution (PBS), the electrochemical activity of g-C3N4/Zn-Co ZIF/GCE toward sertraline was examined. At g-C3N4/Zn-Co ZIF/GCE, sertraline's oxidation peak currents sharply increased and its overpotential considerably dropped. By employing differential pulse voltammetry (DPV) to analytically determine the concentration of sertraline, the potential utility of the sensor was shown. These findings are useful for actual sample analysis. The g-C3N4/Zn-Co ZIF/GCE had a detection limit of 0.3±0.01 μM utilizing DPV and operated linearly in the range of 1.0-385.0 μM. Sertraline was effectively detected in actual samples using the manufactured sensor. The tests showed that Zn-Co ZIF and g-C3N4 are suitable electrode materials that increase sensitivity and provide a high surface-to-volume ratio. Here, a novel sensor for measuring sertraline is presented that is easy to use, quick, sensitive, and economical.

Development of an electrochemical sensor based on ferrocene dicarboxylic acid/Ce-doped SnO2 nanosheets-modified carbon paste electrode for epinine detection

Abstract
A carbon paste electrode (CPE) that was modified with ferrocene dicarboxylic acid (FDC) and Ce-doped SnO2 nanosheets (Ce-doped SnO2 NSs) was created for the purpose of efficient epinine detection. Various analytical methods such as field emission scanning electron microscopy (FESEM) and Fourier transform infrared spectroscopy (FTIR) were applied to determine the chemical composition and surface morphology of the prepared Ce-doped SnO2nanosheets. Furthermore, the modified electrodes (FDC/Ce-doped SnO2 NSs/CPE) were tested in a pH range of 2 to 9 for their respective activities towards epinine detection. The electrochemical technique was applied for the determination of the range and limit of detection. The modified electrode exhibited a linear detection range of 0.1-300.0 µM. Moreover, the LODs for low and high doses were equal to 0.03±0.001 µM, respectively. Among the kinetic properties of epinine in different media, diffusion and transport coefficients were studied. Finally, the modified electrode was tested for its activity on an actual sample.

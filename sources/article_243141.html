Synthesis and investigation of structural and magnetic properties of Fe₂O₃/Fe₃O₄/MWCNT ternary nanocomposite: Application for the detection of the medicine drug epinine

Document Type : Original Article

Authors

Reihaneh Mollashafiee 1
Jamileh Seyed-Yazdi 1
Farnaz Jamadi 2

1 Department of Physics, Faculty of Science, Vali-e-Asr University of Rafsanjan, Rafsanjan, Iran

2 Department of Physics, Sirjan University of Technology, Sirjan, Iran

https://doi.org/10.48309/ajnm.2026.576556.1035
Abstract
The main objective of this project is to develop a new electrochemical sensor which detects epinephrine. Researchers synthesized the FeO₃/FeO₄/MWCNT ternary nanocomposite through a simple process which they used multiple microscopic and spectroscopic methods to determine its crystallinity and content and shape. The researchers studied how the fabricated nanocomposite performed in electrochemical tests which involved morphine detection after they deposited the material onto a screen-printed graphite electrode (SPGE). The SPGE developed through nanocomposite modification showed better electrocatalytic performance than the unmodified electrode when testing for epinine oxidation. The researchers examined the current sensor (Fe₂O₃/Fe₃O₄/MWCNT/SPE) using differential pulse voltammetry (DPV) under controlled laboratory conditions. The research results showed a linear dynamic range which extended from 1.0 to 200.0 µM together with a limit of detection (LOD) which reached 0.4 µM. The predicted sensitivity was 0.1894 µA/ µM. Researchers used the Fe₃O₃/Fe₃O₄/MWCNT for voltammetric measurements of epinine in urine samples which resulted in recovery rates of approximately 100% for all tested samples.

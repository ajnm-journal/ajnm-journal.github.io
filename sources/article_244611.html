Modified voltammetric sensors for measurement of methyl parathion

Document Type : Review Article

Author

Niloufar Akbarzadeh-T

Department of Chemistry, University of Sistan and Baluchestan, P.O. Box 98135-674, Zahedan, Iran

https://doi.org/10.48309/ajnm.2026.583321.1040
Abstract
Population growth of humans leads to expansion of industrial agriculture in which pesticides are an essential component. These chemicals allow the destruction of harmful factors such as weeds, insects, and microbes; that not only damage the health value of foods, but also cause unpleasant effects on human beings. Hence, detection, and quantification of pesticides are vital issues concerning human beings’ health. Since the development of numerous analytical chemical methods for the determination of these organic compounds, electrochemical techniques, considering their sensitivity, selectivity, low cost, and environmental friendliness have received wide interest recently. This paper reviews the research papers that concern the construction and performance of modified electrochemical voltammetric sensors to determine and quantify pesticides.

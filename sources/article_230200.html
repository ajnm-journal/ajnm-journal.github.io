Abstract
Vitamins are a class of organic substances that are essential to the proper functioning of living organisms. Certain vitamins are considered important because, although they can be produced by these organisms, they must be consumed to meet their needs. The human body may develop metabolic diseases as a result of vitamin deficiencies or insufficiencies; therefore, it is critical to monitor vitamin levels and ensure you get enough of them daily through food or supplements. The determination of vitamins is primarily achieved through various analytical methods, including chromatographic, spectroscopic, and spectrometric techniques. Ongoing research aims to develop new and more efficient methodologies for vitamin analysis, with electroanalytical methods, particularly voltammetry, being among the most common. This study focuses on the use of electroanalytical techniques for vitamin determination, highlighting the advancements in voltammetry developed in recent years. Specifically, this review provides a comprehensive bibliographic survey of modified electrode surfaces utilizing carbon nanomaterials for vitamin analysis.

A review of recent advances in the application of MXene-modified electrochemical sensors for the detection of specific drugs

Abstract
The remarkable qualities of MXene, a new family of two-dimensional transition metal carbides, such as its hydrophilicity, high electrical conductivity, huge surface area, and attractive topological structure, have made it a potential material for a number of uses. MXenes are efficient transducers for electrochemical sensing because they can dissolve in ordinary solvents and combine with other nanomaterials to produce composites. According to the literature, MXenes are increasingly being used in the detection of various analytes. An overview of the applications of MXene-modified materials is provided for sensing applications in this study. The analytes are grouped, and a thorough explanation of each category is provided to facilitate the audience’s understanding.

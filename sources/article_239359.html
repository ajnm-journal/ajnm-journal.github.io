Reduced graphene oxide/ZnS nanowires modified screen printed electrode for the quantification of medicinal drug calcium folinate in injection samples

Document Type : Original Article

Authors

Reza Zaimbashi
Ahlam Bazrafkan

Environment Department, Institute of Science and High Technology and Environmental Sciences, Graduate University of Advanced Technolo-gy, Kerman, Iran

https://doi.org/10.48309/ajnm.2025.562139.1023
Abstract
The research reports the development of a new type of modified screen-printed electrode (SPE) incorporating reduced graphene oxide and ZnS nanowires (rGO/ZnS NWs) for highly sensitive electrochemical detection of calcium folinate (CF). The behavior of the rGO/ZnS NWs-based SPE was investigated using cyclic voltammetry (CV), chronoamperometry, and differential pulse voltammetry (DPV). Relative to the bare SPE, the modi-fied electrode demonstrated markedly enhanced electrocatalytic proper-ties and a larger electroactive surface. Under optimized conditions, the rGO/ZnS NWs/SPE provided a wide linear response range (0.2–350.0 μM) along with a very low limit of detection (LOD=0.06 μM). In addition, it successfully measured calcium folinate in injection samples, yielding excellent recovery values (97.8–103.3%). Overall, the results highlight the suitability of the rGO/ZnS NWs-modified SPE as a fast, economical, and dependable tool for electroanalytical applications in pharmaceutical analysis.

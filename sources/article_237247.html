A Novel Ni(II) nano-complex with schiff base ligand: Synthesis, spectroscopic characterization and study of antibacterial properties

Document Type : Original Article

Authors

Mohsen Darvishbeigi
Niloufar Akbarzadeh-T

Chemistry-Science-University of Sistan and Baluchestan - Zahedan- Iran

https://doi.org/10.48309/ajnm.2025.561983.1022
Abstract
A new mononuclear Ni(II) nanocomplex with the formula [Ni(L)2] (a) (L = 2-hydroxy-1-naphthaldehyde-2-aminothiophenol) has been synthesized by a sonochemical process. The nanocomplex (a) was characterized using FT-IR, UV-Vis, elemental analysis, single-crystal X-ray diffraction and scanning electron microscopy (SEM). The average size of the nanocomplex synthesized via the sonochemical method was approximately 52 nm. Spectroscopic analyses revealed that the two Schiff base ligands form an octahedral geometry through the oxygen, sulfur and nitrogen atoms. The antibacterial behavior of the synthesized compound was investigated for two groups of gram-positive members of Staphylococcus aureus (S. aureus ATCC 25923) and Enterococcus faecalis (Enter_faeca ATCC 29212) and two gram-negative members of Escherichia coli (E. coli ATCC 25922) and Pseudomonas aeruginosa (P. aeruginosa ATCC 27853). In this study, the inhibition method and the dilution method were used to determine the minimum inhibitory concentration (MIC) and the minimum bactericidal concentration (MBC), and the results showed that the synthesized complex is active against bacteria.

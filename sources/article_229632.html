Preparation of bimetallic Cu, Zn complex with OPD: Synthesis, characterization, spectroscopic methods, and interaction with FS-DNA

Document Type : Original Article

Authors

Samira Azoogha
Niloufar Akbarzadeh-Tb

Chemistry Science University of Sistan and Baluchestan Zahedan Iran

https://doi.org/10.48309/ajnm.2025.534445.1007
Abstract
In this study, the bimetallic [Zn(OPD)(μ-SCN)Cu(OPD)] (a), where OPD = o-phenylenediamine ligand, was synthesized. The complex (a) was studied by spectral tools, such as FT-IR and UV-Vis spectroscopy. Interaction studies of complex (a) with fish sperm DNA (FS-DNA) were also conducted using fluorescence, UV-Vis, and gel electrophoresis techniques. Additionally, thermodynamic parameters ΔH°, ΔS°, and ΔG° were calculated from these interactions. The results revealed the hydrogen bonding and van der Waals forces in groove binding mode interaction between the complex (a) and the DNA. Noteworthy, the viscosity measurement showed that increased concentration of the complex has no effect on DNA viscosity, suggesting that binding interaction will not produce much distortion of the helical structure of the DNA. Circular dichroism (CD) spectra revealed extensive alteration in the DNA structure upon interaction of the DNA with the complex (a). This means that while the complex unfolds the DNA in coordination without changing its viscosity, it decreases detectable conformational changes by CD spectroscopy. This discovery in two ways illustrates how complex stealthily manipulates DNA structure and indicates the significance of using more than a single analytical method to efficiently distinguish between metal-DNA interactions.

Theoretical investigation of C20 nanocarrier for fluoxetine in biological media

Document Type : Original Article

Authors

Sayed Ali Ahmadi 1
Razieh Razavi 2

1 Department of Chemistry, Ke.C., Islamic Azad University, Kerman, Iran

2 Department of Chemistry, Faculty of Science, University of Jiroft, Iran

https://doi.org/10.48309/ajnm.2025.534797.1009
Abstract
Recently, fullerene nanoparticles have garnered significant interest because of their distinct physical and chemical characteristics. When appropriately modified, these nanoparticles exhibit strong biocompatibility, notable anti-tumor effects, and anti-depressant properties, making them promising for cancer and depression treatment. In this study, density functional theory (DFT) calculations were employed to theoretically analyze the interaction between fluoxetine (F), a medicinal compound, and the C20 surface in both gas phase and physiological environments. The DFT results and adsorption energy data indicate that this nanocarrier performs effectively in biological systems.

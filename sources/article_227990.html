MOF/MWCNTs nanocomposite modified glassy carbon electrode for voltammetric determination of sulfanilamide

Document Type : Original Article

Authors

Zahra Dourandish
Zahra Ghasemia

Environment Department, Institute of Science and High Technology and Environmental Sciences, Graduate University of Advanced Technology, Kerman, Iran

https://doi.org/10.48309/ajnm.2025.533392.1003
Abstract
Sulfanilamide (SFA) is a synthetic antimicrobial chemical that belongs to the sulfonamides class and is frequently used for both therapeutic and preventative purposes in both human and veterinary medicine. The solvothermal method was used in this study to successfully prepare a nanocomposite material of an iron-based metal-organic framework (MIL-101(Fe)-NH₂) with the addition of multi-walled carbon nanotubes (MWCNTs), or MIL-101(Fe)-NH₂/MWCNTs. The prepared nanocomposite was then utilized as the working electrode for the voltammetric determination of SFA after modifying a glassy carbon electrode (GCE) to create the MIL-101(Fe)-NH₂/MWCNTs/GCE. The modified electrode demonstrated notable electrocatalytic activity towards the SFA oxidation compared to the bare GCE. Under optimized experimental conditions, the oxidation peak current of SFA increased linearly with concentration between 0.5 and 100.0 μM, with a computed limit of detection of 0.3±0.02 μM.

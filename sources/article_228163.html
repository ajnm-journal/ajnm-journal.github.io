Antibacterial effects of mixed ligand complex formation of 2-Aminobenzamide with Cu(II) in the presence of glycine

Document Type : Original Article

Authors

Niloufar Akbarzadeh-T
Bahareh Karami

Department of Chemistry, University of Sistan and Baluchestan, P.O. Box 98135-674, Zahedan, Iran

https://doi.org/10.48309/ajnm.2025.533607.1005
Abstract
A mixed ligand of bioactive Cu(II) complex has been synthesized from 2aminobenzamide (2AB) and glycine (gly). Then structurally characterized by various physico-chemical and spectral studies such as FT-IR, UV- Vis methods and elemental analysis. The results showed that the 2-aminobenzamide ligand was coordinated to the metal ion via the N and O atoms. Based on the spectral studies, both the ligands act as bidentates and they chelate with Cu(II) ion to form a stable six, five membered chelate ring with octahedral geometry. In this study, inhibition method and dilution method were used to determine the minimum inhibitory concentration (MIC) and the lowest bactericidal concentration (MBC) and the results showed that the synthesized complex against bacteria are active. The antibacterial behavior of the synthesized compound was examined for two groups of gram-negative bacteria (Escherichia coli) and gram-positive (Staphylococus aureus). The results showed that among the synthesized samples complex showed higher antimicrobial activity than ligands.

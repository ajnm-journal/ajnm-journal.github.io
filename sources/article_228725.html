Killing of breast cancer cells by nano-coated graphene oxide: targeting with folic acid and loading with carvacrol

Document Type : Original Article

Authors

Fariba Garkani Nejad 1
Shahryar Shakeri 2
Hadi Beitollahi 3

1 Department of Chemistry, Graduate University of Advanced Technology, Kerman, Iran

2 Department of Biotechnology, Institute of Science and High Technology and Environmental Sciences, Graduate University of Advanced Technology, Kerman, Iran

3 Environment Department, Institute of Science and High Technology and Environmental Sciences, Graduate University of Advanced Technology, Kerman, Iran

https://doi.org/10.48309/ajnm.2025.534103.1006
Abstract
In this investigation, we developed a graphene oxide (GO) based biocompatible and targeted nanobiocomposite using folic acid (FA) and human serum albumin (HSA) that carvacrol is loaded on graphene sheets. Separately, GO was conjugated to poly l-lysine (PLL) (GO-PLL) and HSA was conjugated to FA (HSA-FA). Then GO-PLL was covalently linked to HSA-FA. SEM showed that GO-PLL sheets were nano-coated by HSA-FA layer (HSA-FA nano-coated GO-PLL). HSA-FA nano-coated GO-PLL was applied for modification of glassy carbon electrode surface. The modified electrode was used for study of MCF7 cancer cells using linear sweep voltammetry method. Results showed a decrease in signal of conjugated folic acid concurrently with increasing the number of MCF7 cells. So, carvacrol was loaded on nano-coated GO-PLL sheets by π-π interactions. Entrapment efficiency (EE%) of carvacrol was 90.8 %. Cytotoxicity effect of carvacrol-loaded nano-coated GO-PLL was evaluated on MCF-7 cell line using the 3-[4,5 dimethylthiazol-2-yl]-2,5-diphenyltetrazolium bromide (MTT) test. The cells were exposed to the free carvacrol, GO, unloaded-nanobiocomposite, and carvacrol-loaded nano-coated GO-PLL for 48 h. The IC50 of free carvacrol and the carvacrol-loaded nano-coated GO-PLL were determined to be 0.8 mg/ml and 1 mg/ml, respectively. The findings of this study demonstrated the potential of the as-prepared GO-based nanobiocomposite in inducing cytotoxicity against breast cancer cells.

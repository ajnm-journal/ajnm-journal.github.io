About the AJNM repository

A connected scholarly archive

This independent repository presents article metadata from AJNM’s publisher website, with links to the original DOI and full-text PDF. It includes the seven published issues in Volumes 1 and 2, retrieved on 22 November 2026. Articles in press are outside this snapshot.

Source and reuse

Article titles, authors, abstracts, keywords, dates, pagination, DOI and PDF URLs are transcribed from the publisher’s article pages. Each record includes its original source URL and retrieval date. The publisher describes published articles as CC BY 4.0; consult each original article for specific credits and third-party material. Text has been converted to plain text for display; scholarly content has not been rewritten.

Coverage and preservation

Counts on this site are calculated from imported records, rather than copied from changing journal statistics. PDF links point to the publisher; this repository does not store PDF copies and is not a long-term full-text preservation service.

Corrections

For changes to the scholarly record, use the publisher contact page. Update this repository only after checking the source record.

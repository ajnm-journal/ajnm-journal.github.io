Glassy carbon electrode modified with Fe3O4/CuS/PANI/RGO nanostructure for efficient electrochemical sensing of mangiferin

Irannezhad, Fatemeh; Seyed-Yazdi, Jamileh

Read full-text PDFResolve DOIPublisher record

Abstract

A Fe3O4/CuS/PANI/RGO nanostructure was created for this investigation. Several methods were used to examine the structure and morphology of the Fe3O4/CuS/PANI/RGO nanostructure, and then as a working electrode for the voltammetric detection of mangiferin (MANG), a glassy carbon electrode (GCE) modified with a manufactured nanostructure (Fe3O4/CuS/PANI/RGO/GCE) was used. The Fe3O4/CuS/PANI/RGO/GCE showed superior electro-catalytic activity for MANG oxidation as compared to unmodified GCE. The limit of detection (LOD) is 0.5±0.02 μM, and under ideal circumstances, the oxidation peak currents of MANG show a linear relationship with its concentration in the range of 1.0–250.0 μM. Lastly, MANG analysis was used to confirm the constructed sensor's applicability in real samples, yielding outstanding results (recovery 98.0–104.3%, and relative standard deviation (RSD) < 3.4%).

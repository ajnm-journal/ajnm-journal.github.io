A modified pencil electrode based on binary transition-metal sulfide nanoparticles for sensitive detection of the herbicide Diuron

Beheshti-Marnani, Amirkhosro; Rohani, Tahereh

Read full-text PDFResolve DOIPublisher record

Abstract

The development of sensitive analytical methods with extremely low detection limits has consistently remained a critical challenge in the control and monitoring of herbicide residues in agricultural food products. In this regard, transition metal sulfide nanostructures can make a significant contribution to the development of effective electrochemical sensors to address this challenge. Needle-shaped CuₓBi₂₋ₓS₃ nanoparticles were employed as an effective electrocatalyst for the sensitive determination of trace amounts of the herbicide Diuron using a graphite pencil based electrochemical sensor. The proposed sensor was successfully applied for the determination of Diuron over a concentration range of 40 to 900 µg/L, with a limit of detection of 17 µg/L and a sensitivity of 0.251 µA·L·µg⁻¹, without any significant interference from coexisting species. Differential pulse voltammetry (DPV) was utilized as the analytical method, and the structure of the synthesized nanocomposite was confirmed by X-ray diffraction (XRD), scanning electron microscopy (SEM), and energy-dispersive X-ray spectroscopy (EDX).

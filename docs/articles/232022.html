Application of a modified graphite screen-printed electrode for sensitive detection of vitamin C

Sarhadi, Hamid; Moshrefi, Sajedeh; Rezaei Zadeh, Batul

Read full-text PDFResolve DOIPublisher record

Abstract

Vitamin C, is a water-soluble vitamin that is found in varying amounts in a wide variety of fruits and vegetables. It is widely used in the food, chemical, and pharmaceutical industries. In this study, a quick, easy, affordable, and efficient electrochemical method was developed for detecting vitamin C. The electrochemical signal of vitamin C was significantly increased at the La2O3/Co3O4 nanocomposite-modified graphite screen-printed electrode (La2O3/Co3O4/GSPE). SEM, or scanning electron microscopy, was used to clarify the surface characterization clearer. Using differential pulse voltammetry, the limit of detection was determined to be 0.2 ± 0.01 µM with a good regression between the concentration and peak current of vitamin C (R2=0.9993) within the range of 0.7 to 450.0 µM. The modified electrode was effectively used to accurately measure vitamin C in actual samples.

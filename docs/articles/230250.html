Removal of methylene blue from water samples using multi-walled carbon nanotube/ ZnCo-zeolite imidazole framework nanocomposite

Mohammadi, Sayed Zia; Arjmand Kermani, Mahdoght; Emambakhsh, Fatemeh

Read full-text PDFResolve DOIPublisher record

Abstract

The ZnCo-Zeolite imidazole framework multi-walled carbon nanotube nanocomposite was prepared for the study. Scanning electron microscopy (SEM), Fourier transform infrared spectroscopy (FTIR), X-ray diffractometry (XRD), and X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS) were used in the characterization of the synthesized nanocomposite. The catalytic performance of the nanocomposite was also examined for its probable adsorptive removal of methylene blue (MB). Experiments were also conducted on the influence of different parameters such as ionic strength, pH, contact time, and initial concentration. Freundlich and Langmuir isotherms were employed to fit the adsorption equilibrium data, and the Langmuir model was the most appropriate. For the MB removal, the maximum sorption capacity was found to be 28.57 mg/g at 298 K. Both pseudo-first order and pseudo-second order diffusion models were employed to determine the adsorption kinetics and the best one was the pseudo-first order model. The as-prepared nanocomposite has high adsorption capacity for MB from water samples based on these data.

Electrocatalytic oxidation of medicinal drug sertraline at a graphitic carbon nitride nanosheets and Zn-Co zeolitic imidazolate framework modified glassy carbon electrode

Jahani, Peyman; Jafari, Maedeh

Read full-text PDFResolve DOIPublisher record

Abstract

As a novel electrochemical platform for sertraline detection, a glassy carbon electrode modified with graphitic carbon nitride nanosheets (g-C3N4) and Zn-Co zeolitic imidazolate framework (Zn-Co ZIF) (g-C3N4/Zn-Co ZIF/GCE) is suggested. Using cyclic voltammetry (CV) studies in 0.1 M phosphate buffer solution (PBS), the electrochemical activity of g-C3N4/Zn-Co ZIF/GCE toward sertraline was examined. At g-C3N4/Zn-Co ZIF/GCE, sertraline's oxidation peak currents sharply increased and its overpotential considerably dropped. By employing differential pulse voltammetry (DPV) to analytically determine the concentration of sertraline, the potential utility of the sensor was shown. These findings are useful for actual sample analysis. The g-C3N4/Zn-Co ZIF/GCE had a detection limit of 0.3±0.01 μM utilizing DPV and operated linearly in the range of 1.0-385.0 μM. Sertraline was effectively detected in actual samples using the manufactured sensor. The tests showed that Zn-Co ZIF and g-C3N4 are suitable electrode materials that increase sensitivity and provide a high surface-to-volume ratio. Here, a novel sensor for measuring sertraline is presented that is easy to use, quick, sensitive, and economical.
